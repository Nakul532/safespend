Findings

  • Held amber #B7791F fails for small text (3.23:1 on paper, 3.64:1 on white) and for white text on an amber fill (3.64:1). It passes for the large stamp, as specified. Proposal: keep #B7791F for stamps, borders and icons; add --held-ink #8F5E15 (4.93:1 on paper) for small HELD labels and reason codes; put ink text, not white, on amber fills (4.80:1).
  • Hairline #E3DED3 is 1.34:1: fine for decorative card edges, but form fields and buttons get a #5B6270 border (6.13:1) so controls meet the 3:1 rule for UI components.
  • Every other pair passes AA for body text in both themes.
