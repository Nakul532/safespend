What we keep

While you play: a random ID in a cookie, so your level progress survives a reload, and the text you send to the agent with the agent's reply, so the engine can replay your level. No name, email or account.

For limits: a daily count of tries per cookie and per hashed IP address. The hash uses a secret that changes every day and is never stored next to anything you wrote.

Only if you tick "Let SafeSpend learn from my attack": the text of that winning attack, the level and the result, under a submission ID shown to you. We scrub it for personal data and a person reviews it before it is used in a future visitor-submitted test set. Never asked for losing attempts. At most 3 a day per visitor and 200 a month in total.
